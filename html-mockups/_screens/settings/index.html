System settings

General

Sections: scene, plate number, damage (required), people affected, other. Up to 3 per section.
Duplicate rule (FR-08)

Nearby reports with the same accident type and close incident times are flagged as possible duplicates. An admin reviews and confirms them; they are not automatically closed.

Phone GPS is often 5 to 20 m off, so some real duplicates may be farther apart. You can still flag those by hand.
Officer arrival check

When the officer taps "I've arrived", the locations match if he is this close to the reporter's pin. GPS supports the review. If the pins differ or accuracy is uncertain, record supporting evidence to approve or ask for another arrival.
